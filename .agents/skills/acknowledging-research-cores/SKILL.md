# Acknowledging research cores

Verified 2026-10-04 against NIH, Indiana CTSI, and IU core pages, and the
RRID registry. Sources are listed at the end.

Acknowledge every core whose work appears in a paper. Cores use
acknowledgments to justify their funding and to win instrument grants. Some
acknowledgments are required.

## What to include

For each core used, a paper should carry:

1. **The core's acknowledgment text**, if it publishes one.
2. **Grant numbers** the core asks you to cite, such as a center grant or an
   S10 instrument grant.
3. **The core's RRID** in the methods, if it has one.
4. **Indiana CTSI award numbers**, if the core is a CTSI core or the work
   used CTSI services.

Then send the core a copy of the paper. See "Report the paper to the core."

## Required acknowledgments

**Required.** NIH-funded work must acknowledge NIH support in publications
and other public products. The acknowledgment names the NIH institute and
award number and carries a disclaimer: "The content is solely the
responsibility of the authors and does not necessarily represent the
official views of the National Institutes of Health" (NIH Grants Policy
Statement 4.2.1 and 8.2.1, per NIH's Communicating and Acknowledging Federal
Funding page).

**Required.** "Investigators who receive Indiana CTSI funding or use its
services must comply with NIH grant acknowledgement and public access
policies." Publications using CTSI services or resources must cite the
appropriate CTSI award and get a PubMed Central ID (Indiana CTSI Citations
and Acknowledgments page). The page gives these numbers, **Observed
2026-10-04**:

| Support | Award |
| --- | --- |
| CTSI pilot funding or use of CTSI core facilities or services, 2023 to 2030 | UM1TR004402 |
| The same, 2018 to 2023 | UL1TR002529 |
| Use of the CTSI Specimen Storage Facility | Also NCRR construction grant RR020128 |

"You must cite all award numbers under which you received support." The
number depends on the years of support (CTSI page). Use the CTSI page's full
wording; do not paraphrase it.

**Required.** The CTSI says the NIH Public Access Policy applies, and that
"only submitting to PubMed Central and receiving a PMCID meets compliance"
(CTSI page).

## Core-specific text

Copy the wording from the core's page at the time you write. Three examples,
**Observed 2026-10-04**:

- **CMG.** **Required** by CMG policy: "The Center for Medical Genomics should
  be acknowledged in all publications resulting from data generated by the
  center." Its suggested text: "Sequencing analysis was carried out in the
  Center for Medical Genomics at Indiana University School of Medicine,
  which is partially supported by the Indiana University Grand Challenges
  Precision Health Initiative" (CMG Publication and Acknowledgment policy).
- **LMIC.** **Required** by LMIC policy: data acquired on the OMX must
  acknowledge NIH grant NIH1S10OD024988-01. "The grant number MUST APPEAR IN
  THE PUBLICATION." LMIC also asks to be acknowledged, and gives one free
  hour of microscope time per publication that does both (LMIC Policies).
- **Cancer center Flow Cytometry Core.** It expects "every user to
  acknowledge the Flow Cytometry Core and the funds that make the core's
  operation possible." Its text names NCI grant P30 CA082709, NIDDK grant
  U54 DK106846, and an S10 instrumentation grant (its policy page).
  The page writes the S10 number as "1S10D012270," missing an "O." NIH
  RePORTER lists the award as 1S10OD012270-01A1, "BD FACSAria System," at
  IU Indianapolis. Cite S10OD012270.

## S10 instrument grants

When an instrument was bought with an NIH S10 grant, acknowledge that grant
in any paper with data from it. **External.** ORIP counts "Publications
Acknowledging S10 Grants" each year (ORIP S10 Fact Sheet 2025). The LMIC
policy says the same: "The NIH is tracking # of publications/grant" (LMIC
Policies, **Observed 2026-10-04**).

**Recommended.** Ask each core whether any instrument you used came from an
S10, and copy the exact number.

## RRIDs

A Research Resource Identifier (RRID) is a stable identifier for a core,
tool, antibody, or organism. The RRID registry gives each core record a
"proper citation." Put it in the methods where the core is named.

Resolve an RRID before citing it. Retired cores keep their RRIDs, marked
"NO LONGER IN SERVICE."

```bash
s=.agents/skills/iu-research-cores-map/scripts/core_directories.py
$s rrid SCR_025533 SCR_011000
```

register itself.

## Authorship

Authorship is a scientific judgment, not a payment term. Cores state their
own expectations, **Observed 2026-10-04**:

- CMG expects authorship for staff "only when these individuals provide
  collaboration on experimental design and/or analysis and interpretation of
  the data." Paying for service "does not preclude or replace authorship"
  (CMG Publication and Acknowledgment policy).
- LMIC: "There is no expectation of authorship from any of the directors"
  (LMIC Policies).
- Cancer center Flow Cytometry Core: co-authorship "is completely at the
  discretion of the user" (its policy page).

**Recommended.** Agree on authorship at the first consultation, when the
core's role is clear. NIH asks that letters of support "stipulate
expectations for co-authorship" (NIH SF424 Forms-I, G.400).

## Report the paper to the core

- CMG asks for "a copy of the publication" for its oversight reports (CMG
  Publication and Acknowledgment policy).
- LMIC asks for a PDF to credit the free microscope hour (LMIC Policies).
- iLab has a publication tracker that links papers to cores (IUSM iLab
  page).

All observed 2026-10-04.

## A template

**Recommended.** Adjust to each core's own wording.

```text
<Core name> at Indiana University (RRID:<id>) performed <service>.
<Core's required text, verbatim.> Data on the <instrument> were acquired
with support from NIH grant <S10 number>. This work used services of the
Indiana Clinical and Translational Sciences Institute, funded in part by
award <UM1TR004402> from the National Center for Advancing Translational
Sciences. <NIH disclaimer.>
```

## Keep this file current

- Re-read the CTSI page each review. Award numbers change with each CTSA
  cycle.
- Re-run the `rrid` command on every RRID in the table. Mark retired ones.
- Add a core's acknowledgment text when its page states one.
