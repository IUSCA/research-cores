# Using IU research cores

Verified 2026-10-04 against IU core websites, IUSM's iLab page, and IU
policy. Sources are listed at the end.

Each core sets its own rules. This skill gives the common pattern and the
questions to ask. The core's own policy page decides.

## Before the first contact

1. **Read the core's page.** Find its services, policies, rates, and request
   route. The `iu-research-cores-map` catalog links each one.
2. **Know the approvals.** A core cannot start work that needs an approval
   you do not have. See "Approvals the core will ask for."
3. **Have a fund number ready, or know who holds it.** See "iLab."

**Recommended.** Ask for a consultation before the first project. Many cores
offer one free to IU users. LMIC offers "Consultation on experimental design
(no fee for IU or CTSI members)" (its Indiana CTSI entry, **Observed
2026-10-04**). The IUSM Center for Electron Microscopy requires one for new
projects, for a fee it refunds "after project completion" (its page,
**Observed 2026-10-04**).

## iLab

Most IU cores take requests, reservations, and payment through Agilent iLab
at https://iu.ilab.agilent.com. These rules come from the IUSM iLab page,
**Observed 2026-10-04**:

- **Register** with IU credentials. Choose the PI you work with. The PI or a
  lab manager approves the request. Registration "may take up to 24 hours."
- **Everyone needs an account.** "All members of a lab or research group"
  must be active registrants, the PI included.
- **More than one lab.** A user may belong to several labs. Each membership
  needs that lab's approval.
- **Fund numbers.** The PI or lab manager assigns fund numbers to members.
  A missing account can be added by hand, and the request routes to the
  account owner. "At Indiana University, the account owner for all sponsored
  program accounts is the PI and the account owner for all non-sponsored
  program accounts is the Fiscal Officer."
- **Split charges.** One service can be split between two accounts by
  percentage.
- **Requests.** "In general it is not necessary to contact the core prior to
  requesting services through iLab," but core policies vary.
- **Estimates.** iLab cannot produce an estimate for someone who has not
  registered.
- **Help.** iLab support is ilab-support@agilent.com.

## Approvals the core will ask for

These rules belong to IU's compliance offices and to each core, and were
read 2026-10-04. A core will ask for the protocol number before it starts.

- **Vertebrate animals.** **Required.** "All animal research, testing, or
  teaching activities at Indiana University must be thoroughly reviewed by
  an IACUC before work can begin" (IU Research Animal Care and Use page).
- **Human subjects.** IRBs review and approve "research involving human
  subjects," and all submissions go through Kuali Protocols (IU Research
  Human Subjects page). Ask the Human Subjects Office whether a study needs
  IRB review.
- **Recombinant DNA and biohazards.** **Required.** Work with recombinant or
  synthetic nucleic acids, viral vectors, transgenic or knockout animals,
  infectious agents, or human tissues and fluids "must go through a review
  process before work can begin." The PI submits an IBC protocol (IU
  Research Institutional Biosafety Committee page).
- **Biosafety at the core.** **Required.** At the cancer center Flow Cytometry Core,
  "Unfixed BSL2 cells must have an IBC protocol approval number" entered with
  each reservation, and the core's room must be in the protocol. The core is
  BSL2 only (its policy page). Other cores set their own biosafety terms.
- **Imaging in IU Health and IUSM facilities.** **Required.** "All studies
  utilizing radiologic imaging resources for research purposes must register
  their studies with the Office for Research Imaging" (IUSM Research Imaging
  page).

## Training and self-use

Self-use cores train each user before granting access.

- LMIC books trained users through iLab, "first-come first served." Night and
  weekend use is "available only to approved, experienced users" (LMIC
  Policies).
- At the IUSM Center for Electron Microscopy, training is billed at the full
  service price "until capabilities on the equipment are confirmed." The
  director may revoke independent use or require retraining (its page).
- PBIF users need clearance from the facility manager and the department
  office, and request training on each instrument (PBIF page).

All observed 2026-10-04.

## Reservations and cancellation

Unused time often costs money. **Required** by each core's policy, for its
own users:

- **LMIC.** Reserve "up to two weeks in advance," and only 4 hours a day
  between 9am and 5pm. Users pay for time used "plus half of the time
  reserved but not used." Reservations not cancelled by 5pm the day before
  are charged (LMIC Policies).
- **Cancer center Flow Cytometry Core.** Time not cancelled 24 hours ahead
  may be charged "at up to 100% of the hourly rate" (its policy page).

## Sending samples

- Read the core's sample guidelines and use its forms. CGB has separate
  submission forms for RNA, ChIP-seq, small genomes, amplicons, and
  methylation work (CGB Sequencing page).
- Some cores want contact before samples arrive. The Center for Proteome
  Analysis asks users to contact it "prior to submitting samples" (its page).
- **Required.** At CMG, "A full payment is expected if data is obtained,
  regardless of whether or not it represents the result expected." Terminated
  experiments need partial payment. If samples miss CMG's quality guidelines
  and you ask it to proceed, "even if the samples fail, payment is expected"
  (CMG Payment policy).
- **Required.** The NMR Facility assumes samples and metadata "will be
  anonymized" with Critical data removed (NMR Policies).

All observed 2026-10-04.

## Rates

**Required.** IU recharge centers may charge only rates that recover
"no more than allowable and allocable costs." Their internal rates "must not
exceed those rates charged to external customers for the same products
and/or services" (FIN-ACC-400).

Cores publish rates in tiers, usually IU, other academic, and commercial.
Some cores give partner institutions or center members a lower tier.

**Recommended.** This repository holds no rates. For a cost estimate, read
the core's current rate page or iLab listing at the time, and ask the core
for a written quote. Do not reuse a figure from memory, an old quote, or a
colleague's budget. Rates change each year, and the tier depends on who pays.

## External users

- **Indiana CTSI partners.** CTSI-designated cores serve investigators at
  partner institutions. CMG and the South Bend Imaging and Flow Cytometry
  Core say they are open to "Indiana University, Purdue University and
  University of Notre Dame" investigators (their pages, **Observed
  2026-10-04**).
- **Registering.** Non-IU users sign up for an iLab account and affiliate
  with their own institution's lab (IUSM iLab page).
- **Billing.** External users "will not be billed through the iLab system."
  The core issues a KFS customer invoice, and a purchase order number can be
  noted on it (IUSM iLab page). CMG asks for "an Indiana University account
  number or purchase order (PO) number" at the start (CMG Payment policy).
- **Companies.** Commercial users pay the commercial or industry tier.
  **Open item.** No IU-wide page says what agreement a company needs before
  using an IU core. Ask the core, and the Office for Research Administration
  (ORA) if the work involves data or intellectual property.

## Confidentiality and who receives data

- CMG "releases samples to the owner only: PI or active member of his or her
  lab." Data goes "only ... to the PI or a person they designate" (CMG
  Confidentiality policy).
- LMIC treats all data as confidential and will not use it "without express
  consent of the individual users" (LMIC Policies).
- **Required.** CMG is a CTSI core. It must report "the name and department
  of investigators making use of the Core, and a list of publications"
  (CMG Confidentiality policy). Expect a core to ask about your publications;
  see `acknowledging-research-cores`.

All observed 2026-10-04.

## Keep this file current

- Re-read each policy page in Sources each review. Policies change with new
  instruments and staff.
- Replace an example core with another when its page drops the policy.
- **Open item.** No IU-wide page sets common core policies for cancellation,
  failed samples, or external use. Each core sets its own.
