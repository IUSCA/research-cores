# Getting help with research cores

Verified 2026-11-10 against the IU pages named in Sources.

Ask the core first. Core staff answer most questions about services,
samples, scheduling, and charges. Go elsewhere only for what the core does
not decide.

## When IU has no core for the work

1. Ask a related core. Core staff know the other cores and the outside
   vendors.
2. Ask the Indiana CTSI about cores at Purdue and Notre Dame. See
   `matching-research-to-cores`.
3. If several funded labs need the instrument, talk to a core director about
   an S10 application. See `budgeting-core-services-in-proposals`.

## What to send

**Recommended.** Put these in the first message to a core. They save a round
of questions:

- The PI, the lab, and the funding source.
- The research question and the measurement wanted, in two sentences.
- The sample type, organism, number of samples, and replicates.
- Approvals in place: animal protocol, IRB, or IBC, with protocol numbers.
- Whether the data is identifiable or clinical.
- The deadline, such as a proposal due date or a paper revision.
- Whether you need a quote, a letter, or both.

Do not send identifiable data or PHI by email. Describe it instead.
