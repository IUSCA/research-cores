# Getting help with research cores

Verified 2026-10-04 against the IU pages named in Sources.

Ask the core first. Core staff answer most questions about services,
samples, scheduling, and charges. Go elsewhere only for what the core does
not decide.

## Who decides what

| Question | Ask | Contact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What a core offers, a quote, samples, scheduling, training, a charge | The core | Its own contact page; see the `iu-research-cores-map` catalog |
| iLab registration, lab membership, missing fund numbers | iLab support | ilab-support@agilent.com |
| An IUSM core, or IUSM core policy in general | IUSM Office of Research Affairs | Through the IUSM Service Cores pages |
| A CTSI service core, CTSI core designation, CTSI citation | Indiana CTSI | ictsi@iu.edu |
| An IU Research core, such as CGB, IUB-EMC, LMIC, or NCF, beyond what the core answers | IU Research CIMS | cims@iu.edu |
| An instrument listed in Research Equipment and Tools | The listed unit; then the database's contact page | https://equipment-tools.research.iu.edu/contact/index.html |
| Animal protocols needed for core work | Animal Research Protections Office | ARPO@iu.edu |
| IRB review for human subjects work at a core | Human Research Protection Program | irb@iu.edu |
| IBC protocols for recombinant DNA or biohazards | Research Safety Compliance Office | IBC@iu.edu |
| Other research compliance questions | Office for Research Compliance | researchcompliance@iu.edu |
| Proposal development help beyond the core's letter | IU Research Development Services (RDS) | RDS request form or ord@iu.edu; see `getting-help-with-proposals` in `research-funding` |
| Proposal budget lines and routing | The unit's grant administrator, or the Office for Research Administration (ORA) | Through the department; ORA contacts are in `getting-help-with-proposals` in `research-funding` |
| Recharge rate rules | Office of the University Controller, University Cost Accounting | Through FIN-ACC-400's contacts |
| Storage, Globus, and the SDA for core data | UITS Research Technologies | The `getting-help-from-research-technologies` skill in `research-technologies` |
| Classifying or sharing core data | Research Data Commons and others | The `getting-help-with-research-data` skill in `research-data` |

All contacts **Observed 2026-10-04** on the pages in Sources.

## When IU has no core for the work

1. Ask a related core. Core staff know the other cores and the outside
   vendors.
2. Ask the Indiana CTSI about cores at Purdue and Notre Dame. See
   `matching-research-to-cores`.
3. If several funded labs need the instrument, talk to a core director about
   an S10 application. See `budgeting-core-services-in-proposals`.

## What to send

**Recommended.** Put these in the first message to a core. They save a round
of questions:

- The PI, the lab, and the funding source.
- The research question and the measurement wanted, in two sentences.
- The sample type, organism, number of samples, and replicates.
- Approvals in place: animal protocol, IRB, or IBC, with protocol numbers.
- Whether the data is identifiable or clinical.
- The deadline, such as a proposal due date or a paper revision.
- Whether you need a quote, a letter, or both.

Do not send identifiable data or PHI by email. Describe it instead.
